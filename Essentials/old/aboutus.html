📖 About Idara Kitab Ul Shifa

Established in 2000 (1421 Hijri) by Mr. Syed Asad Hussain, Idara Kitab Ul Shifa is an independent publishing house based in New Delhi, India. We are a leading and rapidly growing **Unani medical publishing company**, providing valuable educational resources for **B.U.M.S. (Bachelor of Unani Medicine and Surgery)** students and professionals.

Our catalog includes undergraduate and postgraduate textbooks, ward round handbooks, and essential medical references, helping practitioners enhance their knowledge and expertise.

🌍 Expanding Knowledge in Multiple Languages

We collaborate with healthcare professionals to produce high-quality content, including translations into Urdu, Hindi, and English, making medical knowledge more accessible.

🏆 Recognized Excellence

We have been honored with 11 National Level "Best Publisher" awards and are proud to be **India’s only dedicated Unani medical book publishing company** with a nationwide presence.

📚 What We Publish

  • 📖 B.U.M.S. medical books
  • 📘 General interest books
  • 📗 P.G. M.D. entrance exam preparation materials
  • 📜 Islamic religious and literary works
