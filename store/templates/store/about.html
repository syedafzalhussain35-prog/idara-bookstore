📖 About Idara Kitab Ul Shifa

Established in 2000 (1421 Hijri) by Mr. Syed Asad Hussain, Idara Kitab Ul Shifa is an independent publishing house based in New Delhi, India. We are a leading and rapidly growing Unani medical publishing company, serving B.U.M.S. (Bachelor of Unani Medicine and Surgery) students, scholars, and practitioners across India.

Our publications include undergraduate and postgraduate textbooks, ward-round handbooks, competitive exam materials, and reference works designed to strengthen both academic and clinical knowledge.

🌍 Multilingual Medical Knowledge

We collaborate with renowned healthcare professionals to publish authentic works in Urdu, Hindi, and English, making Unani medical knowledge accessible to a wider audience.

🏆 National Recognition

We have been honored with 11 National-Level “Best Publisher” Awards and proudly stand as India’s only dedicated Unani medical publishing house with a nationwide presence.

📚 What We Publish

  • 📖 B.U.M.S. medical textbooks
  • 📘 P.G. & M.D. entrance preparation books
  • 📗 General & academic publications
  • 📜 Islamic religious and literary works

💙 Commitment to the Unani Community

We actively support authors, editors, and students, while recognizing outstanding contributions to the field of Unani medicine.
