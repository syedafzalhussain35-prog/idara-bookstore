Payment Policy

Effective Date: 1 January 2026

This Payment Policy outlines the terms governing payments made on the website of IDARA KITAB UL SHIFA.

We accept secure payments through trusted payment gateways, including UPI, credit cards, debit cards, net banking, and wallets (if available). Payments are processed via Razorpay or other authorized payment partners.

We are not responsible for transactions declined due to incorrect details, insufficient balance, bank authorization failure, gateway issues, or network problems. Customers should contact their bank or payment provider in such cases.

7. Refunds

Refunds, if applicable, are processed according to our Refund & Return Policy and credited to the original payment method.
