Returns & Cancellations

Effective Date: 1 January 2026

This page is a quick summary of return and cancellation rules. For complete details, refer to our Refund & Return Policy.

1. Return Window

Return requests must be raised within 5 days of delivery and are accepted only for wrong product delivery or transit damage.

2. Return Conditions

  • Item must be unused and in original condition
  • Original packaging and proof of purchase are required

3. Non-Returnable Cases

  • Used, marked, or customer-damaged books
  • Entrance exam books, study materials, and notes
  • Requests after 5 days of delivery

4. Cancellation Rules

Orders can be cancelled only before dispatch. Once shipped, cancellation is not possible.
