Refund Policy

At Idara Kitab Ul Shifa, customer satisfaction is important to us. This Refund Policy explains the conditions under which refunds are processed.

Refunds will be processed to the original payment method.

4. Processing Time

Refunds are usually processed within 3–21 working days, depending on bank or payment gateway timelines.
